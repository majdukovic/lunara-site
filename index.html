Questions

Is Lunara a contraceptive or medical device?

No. Predictions are estimates and must not be used as birth control. Talk to a healthcare professional about medical concerns.

Does it work on hormonal birth control?

Yes. Lunara notes that bleeds are withdrawal bleeds and that phases are estimates, and you can still track symptoms and patterns.

What if my cycle is irregular?

Predictions adapt to the gaps you actually log, and the Insights screen shows your longest and shortest cycles.

Where is my data stored?

Only on your phone. Lunara 1.0 has no account and no cloud, so your cycle data never leaves your device unless you export it.
